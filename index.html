My Story

Every new chapter begins
with an even better story.

Across 3+ years of production engineering, my foundation came from enterprise banking systems, working as an Application Engineer on software used across I&M Bank operations in Kenya, Rwanda, and Tanzania. That work established a practical approach to engineering, with an emphasis on reliability, delivery, and real business impact.

Over time, the focus shifted toward AI engineering, including LLM applications, RAG pipelines, agentic workflows, and automation systems. Work delivered for Julius Baer included enterprise AI assistants that reduced query resolution time by 35%.

Recent work focused on production AI platforms for BBC News, UK, covering knowledge retrieval systems, document ingestion pipelines, intelligent workflows, and Docker-based AWS ECS deployments operating at 99.9% uptime.

That engineering foundation now carries into research: I'm a Robotics & AI MSc student at the James Watt School of Engineering, University of Glasgow, shifting focus toward embodied intelligence, human-robot interaction, and the open questions production work never had time for.
